The story

A life shaped by the water

At first light, the shoreline begins to stir. Boats move across the water while familiar routines unfold on the sand. For generations, people here have learned to read the sea: its changing colors, its quiet warnings, and the seasons that determine what it gives.

But a coastline is never still. The water changes, the weather shifts, and the work that once sustained a family can become harder to predict. What remains is the knowledge people carry with them and the stories they share.

The keepers of the tide understand that the sea is not simply a place to work. It is part of a community's memory, connecting the people who came before with those who will follow.

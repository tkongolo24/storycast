About StoryCast

Stories make space for understanding.

We believe the most meaningful stories help us see the world through someone else's eyes. StoryCast is an editorial storytelling concept built around human experiences, thoughtful listening, and inclusive design.

Explore a story

Our mission

Listen with curiosity. Tell stories with care.

StoryCast brings together narrative writing and audio storytelling to create an experience that feels personal, considered, and easy to explore.

Our editorial approach values context, respect for the people whose stories are told, and clarity for the audience. The aim is not simply to publish content, but to make room for perspectives that deserve to be heard.

Designed for more people

Access is part of the story.

People experience the web in different ways. We aim to support different needs, devices, and ways of consuming a story.

01

Read every story

Written content offers an alternative to listening. Complete transcripts should include all spoken words from the published audio.

02

Navigate with confidence

Semantic HTML, meaningful headings, visible keyboard focus, and a skip link help people navigate the site.

03

Choose your pace

Native media controls let listeners control playback. The layout adapts to smaller screens, and motion preferences are respected.

Our commitment

Accessibility is ongoing work.

We aim to make StoryCast usable with a keyboard, readable on different screen sizes, and understandable with assistive technology. We will test the experience, address barriers, and improve it as we learn.
